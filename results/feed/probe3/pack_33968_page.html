About

Complete Collection of Cleaning Wipes

Speed up your cleaning with the effective formulas you trust.

Choose from:

  • MelaMagic® Heavy-Duty Wipes

    These extra durable, biodegradable wipes take on the toughest and greasiest messes with powerful, plant-derived grease-cutters. Great for grills, stovetops, ovens, and garage messes.

    Purpose

    Tackles the greasiest, grimiest messes indoors and out.

    What to use it on

    Range tops, ovens, garage messes, mechanical parts, decks, outdoor furniture, linoleum or tile floors, concrete, barbecue grills, and anywhere else a greasy stain strikes.

    Replaces

    Heavy duty cleaners and oven cleaners like Easy-Off®, PineSol®, and Mr. Clean®.

    How It Works

    MelaMagic uses a unique blend of plant-derived and natural ingredients, like T36-C5 Melaleuca Oil, to easily cut through tough, greasy messes with industrial strength.

  • Clear Power® Glass Wipes

    Just grab, wipe, and toss. These handy, biodegradable wipes are quick, convenient, and eco-friendly way to clean glass and shiny surfaces, with no harmful chemicals inside.

    Purpose

    Cleans smudges and smears on shiny surfaces.

    What to use it on

    Glass, mirrors, windows, and stainless steel.

    Replaces

    Windex®, any glass cleaner using ammonia.

    How It Works

    Clear Power uses glycolic acid, like that found in sugar beets, to naturally break up and wash away dirt. It dries quickly, leaving a streak and smudge-free shine.

  • Tough & Tender® Wipes

    Just grab, wipe, and toss. These handy, biodegradable wipes are a quick, convenient, and eco-friendly way to clean up messes on the go, with no harmful chemicals inside.

    Purpose

    Cleans up everyday dirt and grime on all surfaces.

    What to use it on

    All surfaces, such as counters, tables, cabinets, appliances, walls, fixtures, and natural stone.

    Replaces

    All-purpose cleaners like Clorox® Clean Up Cleaner + Bleach and Mrs. Meyers® Surface Spray.

    How It Works

    Tough & Tender unlocks the cleaning power of water by reducing its surface tension, which helps the natural cleaning agents penetrate, breakdown, suspend, and completely remove dirt. Tough & Tender's plant-derived, biodegradable cleaners power through messes from sticky fingerprints to oily food spills, to caked on grease.

  • Sol-U-Guard Botanical Convenience Wipes

    Be ready to safely wipe away dirt and grime on hard, no-porous surfaces everywhere you go! Take along your portable packet of 30 pre-moistened biodegradable Sol-U-Guard Botanical Convenience Wipes.

    Purpose

    Wipe away dirt and grime on any hard, nonporous surfaces.

    What to use it on

    Any hard, non-porous surface like door knobs, light switches, and countertops. Do not use on natural marble or stone.

    Replaces

    Products like Lysol® and Chlorine Bleach.

    How It Works

    Sol-U-Guard Botanical Disinfectant uses the natural disinfecting properties of thyme oil and citric acid to break through the lipid envelope and cell membrane that surrounds viruses and bacteria.

  • Rustic Touch® Furniture Polish Wipes

    Rustic Touch cleans, conditions, and protects your wood, leather, and vinyl surfaces. The brilliant shine you loved from the original formula is back, providing protection against scratches and moisture using carnauba wax, the best and hardest natural wax.

    Purpose

    Cleans, conditions, and protects your wood, leather, and vinyl surfaces against nicks and scratches.

    What to use it on

    Wood, leather, laminated surfaces, vinyl, baseboards, leather shoes, faux leather seats and bags.

    Replaces

    Furniture polishes like Pledge® and OrangeGlo®.

    How It Works

    Rustic Touch uses Orange Oil and Carnauba Wax aided by the penetrating power of Melaleuca Oil to give furniture a luxurious finish.
